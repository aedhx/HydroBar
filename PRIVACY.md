# HydroBar Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 13, 2026*

HydroBar is a hydration tracker for the Mac menu bar, made by Antoine Deshoux.

## The short version

HydroBar collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your Mac.

## What HydroBar stores, and where

HydroBar keeps the following on your Mac only, in its own storage:

- the drinks you log: amount, date and time;
- your settings: goal, presets, unit, reminders, active hours, days off;
- your daily goals, so past days are judged against the goal they had.

The desktop widget reads a copy of today's total and your presets through a storage space shared only between HydroBar and its widget.

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The Mac App Store version of HydroBar does not connect to the internet. The direct download contacts GitHub only when you check for updates, to read the latest version number; it sends nothing about you.

## Weight

During setup, HydroBar can suggest a daily goal from your weight. The weight is used for this calculation on your Mac and is never saved.

## Microphone and camera

With **Hold during calls** turned on, HydroBar checks whether any app is currently using your microphone or camera, so that reminders wait until your call ends. HydroBar never turns them on, never receives any sound or image, and stores nothing about your calls.

## Focus

If you add HydroBar's filter to a Focus, macOS tells HydroBar when that Focus starts and ends. HydroBar uses this only to change how reminders appear and which day type applies.

## Exports and backups

When you export to CSV or back up to JSON, HydroBar writes the file where you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling HydroBar deletes its data. You can also use **Reset my day**, or delete individual drinks from today's log.

## Children

HydroBar collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
