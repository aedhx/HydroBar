# HydroBar v2.0.2

## Fixed

- Keyboard shortcuts that use the numeric keypad, the arrow keys, the navigation keys (Home, End, Page Up, Page Down, Forward Delete) or F13 to F20 now show their real name in Settings › Shortcuts. They used to show a code, such as "Key(67)".

---

## Installation

1. Download **HydroBar-v2.0.2.dmg** from the [Releases](https://github.com/aedhx/HydroBar/releases) page, or get HydroBar on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6822246305).
2. Open the DMG and drag `HydroBar.app` into your Applications folder.
3. Open HydroBar. It is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Coming from an earlier version? Replace the old app in Applications: your settings and history are kept.

**Requirements:** macOS 15.1 or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

What arrived in 2.0 and 2.0.1: [2.0](https://github.com/aedhx/HydroBar/blob/main/docs/releases/RELEASE_NOTES_v2.0.md), [2.0.1](https://github.com/aedhx/HydroBar/blob/main/docs/releases/RELEASE_NOTES_v2.0.1.md).
