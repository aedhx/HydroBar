Reminders that know what kind of day you're having

Most reminder apps ping you on a fixed timer. HydroBar plans each reminder from your last glass, your active hours and how far along you are.

  • Only during your active hours, and never after you've reached your goal.
  • A bit more often when you're behind, never more than every 15 minutes.
  • Silent while you're on track. Only the ones that matter make a sound.
  • One reminder on screen at a time, cleared as soon as you drink.

Pick your days

Weekends are days off by default. Four-day week? Choose your own.

One-off days

A holiday or a sick day: choose Today Is a Day Off from the menu bar icon. Tomorrow goes back to normal.

Let your Focus decide

Add the HydroBar filter to your Work Focus, and the Focus you already scheduled tells HydroBar what kind of day it is.

Drinking from a bottle? Just say how much is gone

Turn on bottle mode, set its size, and tap where you're at. HydroBar logs the difference and draws the bottle emptying. Undo puts the water back.

Log a glass from wherever you are on your Mac

HydroBar fits into the tools you already use, so logging never means switching apps.

Keyboard shortcuts

One for each preset, plus Undo and Open HydroBar. They work from any app, and from a Stream Deck too. ⌃ ⌥ 1 and the glass is logged.

Shortcuts, Siri and Spotlight

Six actions: Log Water, Log a Preset, Get Hydration Status, Undo Last Drink, Set Daily Goal and Set Today's Day Type. They return values you can build on.

Widget and Control Center

A desktop widget that stays in sync with the app, and on macOS 26, an Add Water button for Control Center.

Focus filter

Any Focus can turn reminders into a quiet badge in the menu bar, and choose between a work day and a day off.

Raycast and deep links

A Raycast extension, and a URL scheme for everything else: Alfred, Keyboard Maestro, Terminal.

open "hydrobar://add?ml=250"

The menu bar icon

A monochrome ring that matches your menu bar, a checkmark at your goal. Option-click it to log your first preset without opening anything.

Your water intake is nobody else's business

HydroBar has no account, no analytics and no ads. The Mac App Store version can't even connect to the internet.

Everything stays on your Mac
Drinks, goals and settings live in HydroBar's sandbox. Export to CSV or back up to JSON whenever you want.
Your weight is never saved
Setup can suggest a goal from your weight, about 30 ml per kilogram. The number is used once, on your Mac, then forgotten.
Calls stay private
To hold reminders during a call, HydroBar only checks whether an app is using the microphone or camera. It never turns them on and receives nothing.
No permission to grant
Notifications are the only one, asked when you turn reminders on. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Which Macs does HydroBar run on?

Any Mac with macOS 15.1 Sequoia or later, Apple silicon or Intel. A few extras need macOS 26, like the Control Center button.

How much water should I drink?

It depends on you, your activity and the weather. HydroBar can suggest a starting point of about 30 ml per kilogram of body weight, and you can change it anytime, or set a different goal for each day of the week. HydroBar isn't a medical device: ask a doctor about your own needs.

Is there an iPhone version? Does it sync with Apple Health?

Not for now. HydroBar is built for the Mac menu bar, and Apple Health isn't available to Mac apps.

Will my reminders follow me around all day?

No. They stay within your active hours, stop once you reach your goal, wait during calls and while your screen is asleep, and you can pause them for an hour, until this evening or until tomorrow.

Can I count coffee or tea?

For now HydroBar counts amounts, not drink types: log whatever you count as water. Drink types are on the roadmap.

What happens to my data if I change Mac?

Back up to JSON from Settings › Data, and restore it on the new Mac. Restoring only adds what's missing, so doing it twice changes nothing.

Which languages does it speak?

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese. It follows your Mac's language, or the one you pick in Settings.

Is HydroBar open source?

Yes, under the MIT license. The code, the issue tracker and the release notes are on GitHub.
