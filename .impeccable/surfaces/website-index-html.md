# Landing page · hydrobar.adx.cool

Scope: the public landing page, `website/index.html`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: general-public Mac users, mostly at a desk, deciding in seconds whether HydroBar fits their day. Action: install it free from the Mac App Store (DMG on GitHub until the App Store link is live). Proof: the interface itself, demonstrated; no testimonials, numbers or press exist and none are invented.

Constraints: keep the established world (felt-drop blue, macOS interface recreated faithfully, Bricolage Grotesque display with Instrument Sans text, light and dark). English. WCAG AA. Reduce Motion honored.

Chosen structure: the giant ring (dealt card 2 of the surface round). Memorable moment: the menu bar's tiny ring zoomed to page scale, filling hour by hour as the visitor scrolls through a day.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is HydroBar's menu bar ring at a thousand times its size. One ring carries the story from 8 AM to the goal; it refuses the split hero with a product shot and the row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched felt-drop blue #2563c9 for the opening and the close, white ring arc, navy #0f1b2d pace tick and ink, green #1f9d55 only at the goal. Light #f3f6fa sections between, navy in dark mode. Bricolage Grotesque at monumental sizes with tabular numerals; macOS popovers and notifications as faithful plates.

STORY: The visitor sees their day as a ring, watches it fill as they scroll from morning to night while reminders arrive at the right moments, wait during a call and end with a last call, understands it never nags and never leaves the Mac, and taps Get HydroBar free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blue. Ring about 110vh wide, cropped by the right and bottom edges, percentage numeral at about 20vw inside it, pace tick and its time label on the arc. Left column: headline, one line of copy, white primary button "Get HydroBar free" with a secondary scroll link. A thin menu-bar strip across the top carries the nav, the mini ring and the clock.

FORM: Surface structure 2 of 3 dealt (index 3 on my ordered list of seven: the ring as hero object). seed: none (unavailable)

Signature interaction: scroll-scrubbed day. The ring stays pinned while the clock in the menu-bar strip runs from 8:00 to 22:00, the arc fills, notification plates arrive at their times, the 14:00 call holds them, and at the goal the ring turns green with a checkmark. Motion grammar: arcs ease, numerals roll, nothing bounces; Reduce Motion shows the day as discrete stops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
