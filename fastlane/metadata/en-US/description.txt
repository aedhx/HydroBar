HydroBar lives in your menu bar and helps you drink enough water, without getting in your way.

Log a glass in one click, see your day at a glance in the ring, and get reminders that make sense.

LOG IN A SECOND
• Your own presets, one click each
• Hold to Add for any amount
• Bottle mode: just say how much of your bottle is gone
• Keyboard shortcuts, the Shortcuts app, Siri, a desktop widget and Control Center

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOUR DAY
• Only during your active hours, and they stop once your goal is reached
• A little more often when you're behind your pace
• They tell you where you are: "1.2 L of 2 L · 800 ml to go by 10 PM"
• Log a drink or type any amount right from the notification
• They wait while you're on a call or in a video meeting
• They follow your Focus modes, and a last call comes before the day ends

WORK DAYS AND DAYS OFF
Days off get their own hours, reminders and goal. Mark a holiday in one click, add your time off, or let your Work Focus decide.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Streaks, a weekly summary and a monthly recap you can share
• Your year at a glance
• When you drink during the day, and your longest dry spell
• A different goal for each day of the week

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no tracking, no data collected. Everything stays on your Mac. Export to CSV or back up to JSON whenever you want.

Available in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Dutch, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese.

HydroBar gives general hydration reminders. It is not a medical device; ask a doctor about your own needs.
